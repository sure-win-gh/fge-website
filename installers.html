Straight answers

The questions installers actually ask

I already have a parts supplier. Why would I change?
You probably wouldn’t change — most installers keep both and use whoever has the part and the better price on the day. The difference is that we’re the authorised Fröling UK distributor, not a reseller buying stock in, so trade pricing comes off our own list rather than someone else’s marked-up one. Open an account, compare us on your next few parts, and decide from there.
Do I have to become a Fröling installer?
No. A trade account is for any UK biomass installer or service engineer, whatever badge is on the boilers you look after. Joining our approved installer network is a completely separate thing, and it’s entirely up to you whether you ever want to talk about it.
You don’t sell Herz or Binda boilers — can you really supply the parts?
Yes. We hold dedicated parts ranges for Herz — pelletstar, pelletstar CONDENSATION, firematic, firestar, pelletfire and BioFire — as well as Binda, and we source for other brands on request. Ignition components, lambda and O₂ sensors, grates and stoker parts, seals and gaskets, sensors, control boards, fans and motors, fuel feed and heat exchanger parts.
I don’t know the part number. Can you still help?
That’s most calls, honestly. Send a photo, the boiler model number, or just describe what’s failed. Our engineers are factory-trained by Fröling in Austria — they can diagnose faults remotely, identify the part, and advise on fitting, at no extra charge. For other brands we’ll help you pin it down too.
What happens after I submit the form?
An account manager calls you during office hours — Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 5:00pm — to confirm a few details, set your pricing up and answer anything you want to throw at them. It’s a working conversation, not a sales script. In a hurry? Call 0333 700 0275.
Where are you, and do you deliver to me?
We deliver parts and complete systems nationwide across the UK from our stocked warehouse, and we’ve supported over 500 installations UK-wide since 2012. Wherever you’re working, we can get to you.
