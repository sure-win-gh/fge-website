Frequently asked questions
about Herz spare parts

What Herz boiler models do you stock spare parts for?
We stock parts for the full current Herz Energietechnik range including: pelletstar, pelletstar-H and pelletstar-HE (10–105 kW pellet), pelletstar CONDENSATION (80–101 kW), firematic (20–301 kW wood chip), firestar De Luxe and Lambda ECO (18–40 kW log gasification), pelletfire (20–40 kW combination), and BioFire / BioFire Taurus (400–1,500 kW industrial). We can also source parts for older and discontinued Herz models. Call us with your serial number.
Are your Herz parts genuine OEM, not aftermarket copies?
Yes, 100% genuine Herz Energietechnik parts every time. We source directly from Herz in Austria to guarantee full compatibility and authentic quality. We do not supply aftermarket or copy components. Non-genuine parts risk voiding your warranty and can cause premature component failure or system damage.
How do I find my Herz boiler model and serial number?
Your Herz boiler's type plate is typically mounted on the front panel or side of the unit, and shows the full model name (e.g. "Herz firematic 50"), serial number, kW output, and year of manufacture. Your original commissioning documentation should also contain this information. If you're unsure, send us a photo of the boiler or type plate and we'll identify the model for you.
How quickly will my Herz spare parts be delivered?
In-stock Herz parts ordered before 2pm on a working day are dispatched same day, with next working day delivery available across mainland UK. For non-stocked items, we can typically source directly from Herz Energietechnik in Austria within 3–7 working days. For urgent orders, call us on 0333 700 0275 to discuss fastest available options.
Can you help diagnose a fault on my Herz boiler?
Yes. Our technical team has hands-on experience with the complete Herz range. Share the error code shown on the controller display or describe the symptoms (e.g. shutdown behaviour, fault history, unusual noise) and we'll advise on the most likely cause and the parts required. We can also provide on-site service support for more complex faults. Contact us for details.
