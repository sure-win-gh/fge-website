Biomass spare parts:
frequently asked questions

How quickly are biomass boiler spare parts dispatched?
In-stock items ordered before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day for next working day delivery. We hold extensive UK stock of Froling and Herz spare parts to minimise your boiler's downtime. Saturday and express delivery options are also available. Call to arrange.
Are your biomass spare parts genuine OEM parts?
Yes, every part we supply is 100% genuine, original equipment manufacturer (OEM). As an authorised Froling UK distributor since 2012, we supply only genuine Froling spare parts. Our Herz parts are also sourced directly from Herz Energietechnik. Using genuine parts protects your boiler's warranty and ensures maximum safety and performance.
What if I don't know my part number?
No problem at all. Call our technical team on 0333 700 0275, or submit an enquiry with your boiler make, model, serial number, and a description or photo of the part. Our engineers will identify the correct part and advise on installation if needed, at no extra charge.
Do you supply parts for older biomass boiler models?
We stock parts for a wide range of current and legacy Froling and Herz models. If a part is not in our standard UK stock, we can typically source it direct from the manufacturer within a few working days. Contact us with your boiler serial number and we'll confirm availability.
Can heating engineers and installers set up a trade account?
Absolutely. We offer trade accounts for professional heating engineers and installation companies, with preferential pricing, credit terms, and a dedicated account manager. Contact our team on 0333 700 0275 or email info@freedomgreen.co.uk to get set up.
Do you offer technical support alongside parts supply?
Yes. As authorised distributors with over 12 years' experience, our technical team can advise on fault diagnosis, part replacement procedures, and commissioning for most Froling and Herz systems. We also offer on-site service and maintenance. Contact us for details.
